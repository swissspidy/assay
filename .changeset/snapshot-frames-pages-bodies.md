'@swissspidy/assay': minor

Snapshots now cover iframes, other open pages and form uploads. `captureSnapshot` (and so `verifyPage` and `expectOutcome`) fills each `iframe` node with its frame's content, same-origin or cross-origin, named by the iframe's title, with the frame's URL as its `url`; locators find frame content like any other node, and `{"role": "iframe", "name": …}` scopes a check to one frame. A contenteditable title field (like the WordPress block editor's) now has its text as its value. Other open tabs and popups are kept as `pages`, and the snapshot's page is the active one: the page passed in, or a popup or tab it opened that was opened or navigated more recently. A new `page` assertion (`{"kind": "page", "match": {"url"?, "title"?}, "assert": …}`) reads another open page; no matching page, or several, abstains. Multipart request bodies are parsed into fields like URL-encoded ones, uploads are recorded as `{filename, type, size}` without their content, and form bodies keep their text as `requestBodyRaw`; a body that was sent but could not be read makes `requestBody` checks abstain instead of fail. `frames: false` and `pages: false` keep the previous capture. Snapshots stay `assay.snapshot/v1`, and stored snapshots load and evaluate as before. Stress testing adds an unloaded-frame mutant, mutants on the selected page, and extra-frame and extra-page variants for snapshots that have frames or several pages.
